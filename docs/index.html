Frequently asked questions

 

Is Toran a one-time purchase or a subscription?

A one-time purchase. You pay once, download the software, and keep your installed version forever on your own server.

Can wedding planners use it for clients?

Yes, on the Planner and Agency plans. Both include white-label branding and free installation.

How are WhatsApp invitations sent?

Through the official WhatsApp Cloud API (Meta), Twilio, or free click-to-chat links, with throttling, retries and automatic opt-outs.

Do guests need an app to RSVP?

No. Each family opens its own private link on any phone. No login is needed.

What hosting do I need?

PHP 8.2+, MySQL 5.7+ or MariaDB 10.3+ and one cron job, on cPanel or Plesk shared hosting, a VPS, or Docker. We can install it for you.

Can I try it first?

Yes. Open the live demo and pick Admin, Planner or Staff. No sign-up is needed, and the sample wedding reloads every hour. Or book a guided demo.
