## 1.0.0 (9 November 2026): first release

**Planning**
- Celebrations (wedding, janoi, seemant, festival and more) with functions, venues, dress codes and a main event
- Guests and families with sides, diets, tags and per-function invitations; Excel/CSV import with column mapping and preview; Excel export; bulk actions
- RSVP: a private link per family (no login), per-function answers with headcount and food preference, WhatsApp reminders
- Shagun ledger with running balance, totals by function, side and mode, and Excel/PDF export
- Tasks, plus a dashboard with countdown, RSVP progress, shagun, timeline, payments due and check-in status

**Invitations and WhatsApp**
- Invitation designer with Indian-script fonts, placeholders and three sample designs; a personalised image for every family
- A signed check-in QR code for each family, printed on its invitation and shown on its RSVP page
- WhatsApp sending through click-to-chat, Meta Cloud API or Twilio, with campaigns, throttling, retries, delivery receipts, a duplicate guard and opt-outs

**Seating, vendors and budget**
- Drag-and-drop floor plan per function, capacity rules, keep-families-together auto-seating, CSV/PDF export
- Vendor directory (nine categories), quotations and contracts, advance and instalment payments, direct expenses
- Budget per category (planned vs spent), overdue highlights, payment reminders, Excel/PDF export

**Event day**
- QR check-in desk for phones and tablets with offline sync, live counters, table and diet lookup, and a staff-only role

**Administration**
- Browser installer, system health, white-label branding, English / Hindi / Gujarati interface, role permissions
